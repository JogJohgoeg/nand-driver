02 · Circuit step一步电路多少钱Cost of one circuit step

对三个已烧录的有状态电路做 eth_estimateGas（零输入模式，区块 121591371）。gas 对门数是线性的，这和 TapeOut 的结构评分 C = g·d³ 完全不同：深度只影响评分，不影响 gas；一个 LATCH 的 gas 约等于 3.5 个 NAND。eth_estimateGas on three burned stateful circuits (zero-input pattern, block 121591371). Gas is linear in element count, which is entirely different from TapeOut's structural score C = g·d³: depth affects only the score, not gas; one LATCH costs about as much gas as 3.5 NANDs.

已测的两项合并为 1.311 倍（3,170,826 gas）。合起来的现实预期：解释器本身 3 到 5 倍，热循环静态翻译后 10 倍以上，写密集负载再从脏页和固定项拿 2 到 3 倍。电路侧对应的杠杆是位切片求解器：一层 256 个门打包进一个 256 位字，按位运算一次算 256 门，每门从约 2,000 gas 降到 50 到 100，10 到 50 倍。The two measured levers combined give 1.311× (3,170,826 gas). Realistic combined expectation: 3–5× from the interpreter itself, 10×+ on hot loops after static translation, and another 2–3× from dirty pages and the fixed term on write-heavy workloads. The circuit-side counterpart is a bit-sliced evaluator: pack a 256-gate layer into one 256-bit word and evaluate 256 gates per bitwise operation, cutting each gate from about 2,000 gas to 50–100, i.e. 10–50×.

05 · Where it stops优化救不了的事What optimization cannot fix

同一把尺子量一个大模型。DeepSeek v4.1 Flash 按官方配置精算：每次 decode 的 MoE 矩阵乘加 99.1 亿次，触及路由专家权重 4.51 GB；rv32ima 上实测 FP4 对乘加 20 条指令：Measure a large model with the same ruler. 存储不是墙，算力差七个数量级。所有杠杆加起来不到 100 倍。大模型"在 TapeOut 上运行"只有一种成立的定义：链下执行、轨迹承诺、交互式二分，由链上这台逐位精确的 rv32ima 机器裁决争议的那一条指令。优化解释器的意义，是让裁判那一步和小模型试点便宜十倍。Storage is not the wall; compute is seven orders of magnitude short. All levers combined give less than 100×. There is only one valid sense in which a large model "runs on TapeOut": off-chain execution, trace commitment and interactive bisection, with this bit-exact on-chain rv32ima machine arbitrating the single disputed instruction. The point of optimizing the interpreter is to make that arbitration step and small-model pilots ten times cheaper.

06 · Method方法与复现Method and reproduction

  1. 电路 step：对 #280、#283、#284 的 step() 做 eth_estimateGas，零输入模式加非零模式共 18 组，扣除交易固有 gas 后做三点线性拟合。Circuit step: eth_estimateGas on step() of #280, #283 and #284, 18 runs across zero-input and non-zero patterns, then a three-point linear fit after removing transaction intrinsic gas.
  2. RISC-V 机器：从公开收据的 Ticked(flips, instructions, loadedPages, dirtyPages, gasUsed, halted, cutFlips) 事件取 80 笔样本做最小二乘回归，留出集校验。RISC-V machine: least-squares regression over 80 samples taken from Ticked(flips, instructions, loadedPages, dirtyPages, gasUsed, halted, cutFlips) events in public receipts, validated on a holdout set.
  3. 离线孪生：拉取机器合约 14,490 字节和页合约 1,025 字节的部署字节码，在本地 EVM 里逐字节重放，对照区块 120503146 和 120503286 的 cpuWords() 与脏页指针，8/8 CPU 字、12 加 16 个脏页全部一致。杠杆的实测就在这个孪生上做。Offline twin: fetch the deployed bytecode of the machine contract (14,490 bytes) and the page contract (1,025 bytes), replay it byte-exact in a local EVM, and compare cpuWords() and dirty-page pointers at blocks 120503146 and 120503286: 8/8 CPU words and 12 + 16 dirty pages all match. Lever measurements are made on this twin.
  4. 全程只读 RPC，零交易，零烧录。机器源码未验证，解释以静态反汇编为准。Read-only RPC throughout: zero transactions, zero burns. The machine's source is unverified; interpretation relies on static disassembly.
