Used by VS Code’s built-in Seti file icon theme.
Only 18 icons are included. SVG paths are preserved; viewBoxes are tightened for consistent 16px sizing. Colors use the Seti palette.
The ZIP icon’s white knockout shapes are encoded as a luminance mask so its zipper remains visible when tinted.
