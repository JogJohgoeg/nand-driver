OUR FIRST CHIP · BARD

The Bard

An AI that tells short stories, made directly into a chip. There is no processor and no program inside: what it learned is carved into the silicon, and it starts talking as soon as it powers on. Its complete blueprint is already on chain, where anyone can fetch and check it and no one can change it.
